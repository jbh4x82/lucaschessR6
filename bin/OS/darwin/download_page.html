What is in it

The full program, with the FasterCode engine compiled for arm64 and {{ENGINE_COUNT}} of the bundled chess engines built from their own sources, among them Stockfish 18 and Lucas Monge's Irina. Settings, games and results are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/Lucas Chess R6, so removing the app leaves your data untouched.
